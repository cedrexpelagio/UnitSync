# S1 (Battalion & Brigade S1) Knowledge Base - UnitSync

## Scope & Differences Between S1 Roles
- **Battalion S1 (up to 2 accounts):** 
  - First-level attendance approver.
  - Can import cadets and officers via CSV.
  - Manages company/platoon assignments across the entire corps.
- **Brigade S1 (1 account):** 
  - Second-level (final) attendance approver.
  - Reviews submissions that have already received Battalion S1 approval.
  - Final approval locks the attendance records for official reporting.

## Navigation & Pages
1. **Dashboard** (`/s1/dashboard.php`):
   - Shows overall brigade statistics: total cadets, active companies/platoons, overall attendance percentage.
   - Summarizes pending attendance reviews awaiting your level of approval.

2. **Import Cadets** (`/s1/import_cadets.php`):
   - Bulk upload cadet records from a CSV file.
   - Provides a preview step to detect format errors, duplicate student numbers, or missing mandatory fields.
   - Confirms newly created vs updated cadets.

3. **Import Officers** (`/s1/import_officers.php`):
   - Bulk upload Company Commanders and Platoon Leaders via CSV.
   - Automatically links active Platoon Leaders with login user accounts.

4. **Cadet Roster** (`/s1/roster.php`):
   - Comprehensive roster of all cadets in the unit.
   - Multi-field filters: Company, Platoon, Academic Program, and Status.
   - Click "Edit" to reassign a cadet to a different company or platoon, or assign unassigned cadets.

5. **Officer Roster** (`/s1/officers.php`):
   - List of all assigned officers, their command slots, and linked accounts.

6. **Review Attendance** (`/s1/review_attendance.php`):
   - Central approval queue for platoon attendance submissions.
   - Filter by term, session, company, and submission state.
   - Click a session to inspect individual cadet marks (`review_session.php`).

7. **View Attendance** (`/s1/view_attendance.php`):
   - Analytical view of approved attendance records across all units and programs.

## Attendance Approval Workflow
1. A Platoon Leader submits attendance. State becomes **Submitted**.
2. **Battalion S1 Review:**
   - Battalion S1 inspects the marks, late minutes, and excuse reasons.
   - Battalion S1 clicks **Approve** (marks battalion_approved_by).
   - If issues exist, Battalion S1 clicks **Return to Leader** with constructive return remarks.
3. **Brigade S1 Review:**
   - Brigade S1 reviews submissions that have received Battalion S1 approval.
   - Brigade S1 clicks **Approve** (marks brigade_approved_by).
   - State becomes **Approved** (locked). The submission is now official and included in all cadet attendance percentages.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Why can't I approve this submission?
- **Battalion S1:** You can only approve if the submission is currently in `submitted` state and has not yet been approved by Battalion S1. If already approved at your level, you must wait for Brigade S1.
- **Brigade S1:** You can only approve after Battalion S1 has approved first. You cannot bypass the Battalion S1 review.
- **Already Locked:** If both levels have approved, the record is locked against further modifications.

### How do I return a submission to the Platoon Leader?
- On the `review_session.php` page, click **Return**.
- Type a clear explanation in the **Return Remarks** field (e.g. "Cadet Cruz was marked Absent but has an approved excuse slip").
- The submission state returns to `returned`, notifying the Platoon Leader to revise and resubmit.

### How do I assign or reassign an unassigned cadet?
- Go to **Cadet Roster** (`/s1/roster.php`).
- Filter by "Unassigned" or search the cadet by name or student number.
- Click **Edit**, choose the proper Company and Platoon from the dropdowns, and save.

### What happens when an attendance CSV is imported?
- S1 imports only add/update cadet profiles and rosters. Attendance marks themselves are recorded by Platoon Leaders per session to maintain accountability and the approval chain.
